## Problem

NIE juniors have questions about internships, electives, and hackathons that seniors could answer quickly, but they may not know whom to ask. Build a senior-junior mentor finder where seniors list the topics they can help with and juniors can find and request a mentor. The solution should be based on the needs of real NIE students or staff rather than a generic online answer.

## Essential privacy constraint

No phone number or personal email may ever be shown publicly. Contact details may only be shared after the senior accepts a request.

## Required discovery

- Speak with at least two real NIE students or staff who face this problem.
- Record each interviewee's role, not their phone number.
- Capture one quote from each person.
- Ask permission before interviewing or photographing anyone.

## Prototype requirements

- Build a working prototype of the core mentor-finding feature. A web app, mobile app, or no-code tool is acceptable.
- The prototype must run; a simple working version is sufficient.
- Demonstrate the privacy constraint in the prototype, even if implemented in a basic way.

## Submission checklist

- [ ] Link to the app
- [ ] Code/repository link (GitHub or Drive)
- [ ] Two-minute demo video
- [ ] Short README describing what the app does and which AI/tools were used
- [ ] One-minute video from each member describing their work and learning
- [ ] Contribution log showing who did what and when, in the format specified by the Rules and Guidelines
- [ ] Interview notes: role and one quote for each of at least two participants

## Evaluation emphasis

Submission (60%): understanding of real users, whether the prototype works, handling of the special privacy requirement, and a clean, explainable build. Participation (40%): game plan, progress update, and contribution log. Individual scores account for each member's year; interview questions may cover any teammate's work.
